Driver-First Operational Scenarios
\begin{redbox}[Contingency & Driver Support Scenarios]
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{6-Day driver needs a weekend off:}
\textit{Driver-First Solution:} Allow a 5-day M-F driver to pick up an extra shift at an overtime bonus rate. \textit{Impact:} Costs a premium in payroll, but builds immense loyalty and prevents burnout.
\item \textbf{High volume spike on a route:}
\textit{Driver-First Solution:} Dispatch a ``rescue'' using a driver who finishes early, paying a per-stop bonus. \textit{Impact:} Prevents driver exhaustion; rewards efficient drivers with extra cash.
\item \textbf{Driver calls out sick on a weekend:}
\textit{Driver-First Solution:} Rob (Owner) covers the route, or route volume is split across remaining drivers with bonus pay. \textit{Impact:} Service levels maintained without forcing an off-duty driver to come in.
\item \textbf{Fatigue sets in for 3-Week Rotators:}
\textit{Driver-First Solution:} Introduce a ``Flex Day'' once a quarter where the rotator gets a full 2-day weekend off. \textit{Impact:} Short-term staffing crunch, but lowers turnover for hard workers.
\item \textbf{Truck breakdown mid-route:}
\textit{Driver-First Solution:} Maintain the spare truck at the terminal. Dispatch it immediately to the driver's location. \textit{Impact:} Minimal delay for the driver; prevents them from working late into the evening.
\end{itemize}
\end{redbox}
